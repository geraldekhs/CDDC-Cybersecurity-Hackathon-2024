# CDDC Cybersecurity Hackathon 2024
* The Cyber Defenders Discovery Camp (CDDC) is an annual Capture-the-Flag (CTF) style cybersecurity hackathon where teams compete to solve a variety of security challenges. The objective of each challenge is to obtain a unique keyword or “flag” which can be submitted to earn points. The challenges span multiple categories such as Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT), Binary Exploitation, Digital Forensics, Reverse Engineering etc.
* After a gruelling 30 hours of solving capture the flag challenges, my team (@dlhw, @shinbeann) and I placed **120th out of 771 teams**!
